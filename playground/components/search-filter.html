    No results

    Keep the criteria and recovery together

    The query stays in the form while the result region becomes a compact Product State with a Reset action.

    Stack without changing meaning

    The query spans the form at medium widths. All controls and actions stack into one readable column on compact screens.

    Usage guidance

    Make every applied criterion reversible.

    Preserve the active state in URLs, Pagination, and sorting. Keep one clear Reset path whenever criteria can produce no results.
